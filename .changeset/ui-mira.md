Restyle every shadcn-vue component to the Mira scale and ship the theme as `@mts241alikhlash/ui/theme.css`. An app replaces the copy of its tokens in `src/style.css` with `@import '@mts241alikhlash/ui/theme.css';` (after `tailwindcss` and `tw-animate-css`). The theme holds the colors (primary is now the logo navy `oklch(0.37 0.105 261)`, ring and sidebar primary follow it, `--sidebar` follows `--background`, new `--brand-gold`), the radius scale and new size tokens: `--control-h-xs|sm|md|lg` (20, 24, 28, 32 px on desktop, 24, 32, 36, 40 px below `md`), `--text-control` and `--icon-control`. Components read them with a Mira fallback, so an app still on its old `style.css` renders at the Mira desktop sizes.

Visible changes: buttons, inputs, selects and tab strips are 28 px high on desktop (was 36 to 40 px) with 12 px text, badges are 20 px pills, dialogs and dropdown rows are tighter, tables, alerts and helper text use the control text size, progress is a 4 px bar, focus is a 2 px `ring-ring/30`. `FloatingField` and `FloatingLabelField` use the same 28 px control with a 10 px floated label. Fields stay transparent (no Mira fill). `Button` gets the `xs`, `icon-xs`, `icon-sm` and `icon-lg` sizes. `Dialog`, `AlertDialog` and `Popover` keep their default width and padding where apps rely on it. `SelectContent` keeps `position="popper"` and `Checkbox` keeps the indeterminate state.

Added the shadcn-vue components that were missing: accordion, aspect-ratio, attachment, bubble, button-group, carousel, code-block, combobox, context-menu, drawer, empty, hover-card, input-group, input-otp, item, kbd, marker, menubar, message, message-scroller, navigation-menu, number-field, pin-input, questionnaire, radio-group, resizable, slider, spinner, tags-input, toggle, toggle-group and trace. They are exported from `@mts241alikhlash/ui/<name>` and from the package root, except `code-block`, which pulls `shiki` and is import-only by path. New dependencies: `embla-carousel-vue`, `shiki`, `vue-input-otp`.
