Restyle every shadcn-vue component to the Mira scale and ship the theme as `@mts241alikhlash/ui/theme.css`. An app replaces the copy of its tokens in `src/style.css` with `@import '@mts241alikhlash/ui/theme.css';` (after `tailwindcss` and `tw-animate-css`). The theme holds the colors (primary is now the logo navy `oklch(0.37 0.105 261)`, ring and sidebar primary follow it, `--sidebar` follows `--background`, new `--brand-gold`), the radius scale and new size tokens: `--control-h-xs|sm|md|lg` (20, 24, 28, 32 px on desktop, 24, 32, 36, 40 px below `md`), `--text-control` and `--icon-control`. Components read them with a Mira fallback, so an app still on its old `style.css` renders at the Mira desktop sizes everywhere, phones included (28 px controls). Bump `ui` and switch `style.css` to `theme.css` in the same app change so phones get the larger sizes.

Visible changes: buttons, inputs, selects and tab strips are 28 px high on desktop (was 36 to 40 px) with 12 px text (inputs and textareas stay 16 px on phones so iOS does not zoom), badges are 20 px high with the `rounded-sm` radius of the Mira `xs` button, the `ActionCell` menu is a plain compact dropdown (no fixed 190 px width, 12 px text rows), dialog padding goes from `p-6` to `p-4` and dialog titles from 18 px semibold to 14 px medium, dropdown rows are tighter, tables, alerts and helper text use the control text size, progress is a 4 px bar, buttons get a 2 px `ring-ring/30` on keyboard focus, while fields (input, textarea, select and the like) only change their border color, with no ring or shadow, also when invalid. `FloatingField` and `FloatingLabelField` use the same 28 px control with a 10 px floated label. Fields stay transparent (no Mira fill). `Button` gets the `xs` and `icon-xs` sizes; destructive buttons and badges stay solid red and the outline button keeps the page background with a muted hover. `Dialog`, `AlertDialog` and `Popover` keep their default width. `SelectContent` keeps `position="popper"` and `Checkbox` keeps the indeterminate state.

Added the shadcn-vue components that were missing: accordion, aspect-ratio, attachment, bubble, button-group, carousel, code-block, combobox, context-menu, drawer, empty, hover-card, input-group, input-otp, item, kbd, marker, menubar, message, message-scroller, navigation-menu, number-field, pin-input, questionnaire, radio-group, resizable, slider, spinner, tags-input, toggle, toggle-group and trace. They are exported from `@mts241alikhlash/ui/<name>` and from the package root, except `code-block`, which pulls `shiki` and is import-only by path. New dependencies: `embla-carousel-vue`, `shiki`, `vue-input-otp`.
